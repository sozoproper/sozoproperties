BWEYOGERERE / UGANDA
CHAPTER I — THE BEGINNING

Roots of Resilience.

Born in 1995 in Bweyogerere, Wakiso District, Uganda, Umaru Kaye experienced life's challenges from an early age. The loss of his father during childhood shaped his sense of responsibility and determination.

CHAPTER II — THE HUSTLE

Learning to Build.

His entrepreneurial journey began with street vending and small-scale commerce. Those experiences provided valuable lessons about people, persistence and business.

CHAPTER III — THE VISION

Beyond Limits.

Through experience and ambition, he gradually moved into real estate, developing the entrepreneurial foundation that would shape his future ventures.
